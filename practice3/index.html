The Best Games of All Time

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998)

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998) revolutionized the action-adventure genre by successfully translating a beloved 2D franchise into a fully realized 3D world. Players control Link as he travels through the vibrant land of Hyrule, solving intricate puzzles, exploring vast dungeons, and battling iconic monsters.

Grand Theft Auto IV

Grand Theft Auto IV marked a massive leap forward for Rockstar Games, bringing the sprawling, crime-ridden streets of Liberty City to life with unprecedented realism. The game follows Niko Bellic, an Eastern European immigrant who arrives in America chasing the elusive promise of the American Dream, only to find a dark underworld of debt and betrayal. Rockstar's Euphoria physics engine revolutionized character movement and vehicular damage, making every car crash and pedestrian interaction feel uniquely dynamic. The narrative is widely considered one of the best in gaming history, offering a gritty, mature, and emotionally resonant story about loyalty and consequence.
